How the Caesar Cipher Works

  • Each English letter moves forward by the selected number of places.
  • Decryption moves letters backward by the same amount.
  • Capitalization, spaces, punctuation, and other characters are preserved.
  • This classic cipher is easy to break and is only for learning, not security.
